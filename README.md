# How a stick shift works

An interactive, single-page animation that explains a manual gearbox to kids (7–12). It has three gears (1, 2 and reverse) plus neutral.

Live: https://salalaslam.github.io/gearbox-kids/ (also https://gearbox-kids.vercel.app)

- **Gear stick:** drag the red ball, click 1 / 2 / R / N, or press the keys `1`, `2`, `R` and `N`.
- **Gearbox x-ray:** a shift fork slides the gears along the wheel shaft until their teeth catch an engine gear. The wheel shaft turns orange when it spins.
- **Clutch:** the "hold clutch" button (or `C`) keeps the clutch pressed, so you can shift and then let go.
- **Slow-mo:** `S`. **Engine on/off:** `E`.


# How a hand riveter works

A second demo in the same style shows a pop rivet joining two metal sheets on a closed box, where only the front can be reached.

Live: https://salalaslam.github.io/gearbox-kids/rivet/

- **Steps:** push the rivet in, put the riveter on, squeeze a few times, the stem snaps, pull the riveter away. The big button (or `Space`) does the next step.
- **Squeeze:** drag the riveter's black handle, or press the button. The jaws grab the stem and pull it back, and they slide forward again when you let go.
- **X-ray:** the ball on the end of the stem squashes the rivet's tail into a bulb, which pulls the sheets together until the stem breaks at its thin neck.
- **Wiggle test:** `W`. **Slow-mo:** `S`. **Start again:** `R`.

Each demo is one HTML file with no build step and no dependencies. Open `index.html` or `rivet/index.html` in a browser.
